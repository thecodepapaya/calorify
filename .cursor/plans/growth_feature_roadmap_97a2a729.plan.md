# Growth And Retention Feature Roadmap

## Current Product Shape

Calorify is an AI-first calorie tracker. The strongest existing surfaces are:

- AI photo/text meal logging in [`app/lib/features/log/log_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/log/log_screen.dart) and [`app/lib/core/repositories/food_repository.dart`](app/lib/core/repositories/food_repository.dart)
- A 13-step onboarding flow in [`app/lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart)
- Daily dashboard cards for AI summary, goals, macros, charts, and meal log in [`app/lib/features/home/home_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/home/home_screen.dart)
- Meal reminders in [`app/lib/core/services/notification_service.dart`](app/lib/core/services/notification_service.dart)
- AI summaries generated by backend cron in [`backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts`](backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts)
- Favorites, history, Health Connect, CSV export, feedback/rating prompts, analytics, and Wear OS companion support

The biggest product gap is not the lack of logging capability. It is the lack of a durable value loop after logging: first-meal activation, habit formation, personalized coaching, smart re-engagement, and social/shareable proof of progress.

## Recommended Features

### 1. Guided First Meal Flow

After onboarding completes, take the user directly into a guided first log instead of dropping them on the dashboard. This should be a lightweight flow: choose photo/text, analyze, confirm, then show an immediate “what this means for today” result.

Why this matters:

- Shortens time-to-value.
- Turns onboarding data into a real calorie/macro target immediately.
- Creates the first successful meal log, which unlocks dashboard value.

Likely leverage points:

- [`app/lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart)
- [`app/lib/features/log/log_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/log/log_screen.dart)
- [`app/lib/features/home/widgets/bottom_sheet/`](app/lib/features/home/widgets/bottom_sheet/)
- [`app/lib/core/constants/analytics_events.dart`](app/lib/core/constants/analytics_events.dart)

### 2. Smart Coaching Summary

Upgrade the existing AI summary card from a retrospective summary into a daily/weekly coaching surface. It should answer: “What should I do next?” Examples: add protein at dinner, reduce late snacks, you are under calories but low on fiber, repeat yesterday’s balanced lunch.

Why this matters:

- Gives value even when the user is not actively logging.
- Makes the app feel like a nutrition coach rather than a tracker.
- Builds on existing backend AI summary infrastructure instead of starting from scratch.

Likely leverage points:

- [`app/lib/features/home/widgets/ai_summary_card.dart`](app/lib/features/home/widgets/ai_summary_card.dart)
- [`app/lib/core/repositories/food_repository.dart`](app/lib/core/repositories/food_repository.dart)
- [`backend/src/services/aiSummaryService.ts`](backend/src/services/aiSummaryService.ts)
- [`backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts`](backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts)

### 3. Logging Streaks And Consistency Score

Add a retention layer around consistency, but frame it around useful behavior instead of guilt. Example: “Logged 5 of last 7 days,” “Breakfast consistency improved,” or “You usually miss dinner logging.”

Why this matters:

- Helps users build a habit.
- Gives them a reason to return daily.
- Works well with reminders and weekly recaps.

Likely leverage points:

- Local meal history in [`app/lib/core/db/`](app/lib/core/db/)
- Dashboard widgets in [`app/lib/features/home/home_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/home/home_screen.dart)
- History/favorites providers under [`app/lib/core/providers/`](app/lib/core/providers/)

### 4. Adaptive Reminders

Move beyond fixed meal reminders. Use recent behavior to trigger smarter nudges, such as “You usually log lunch by 2pm,” “No meals logged today,” or “Want to re-log yesterday’s breakfast?” Notification taps should deep-link into the right logging action.

Why this matters:

- Fixed reminders become noise quickly.
- Adaptive reminders are more relevant and can recover missed logging days.
- The current notification service already has scheduling and FCM basics, but tap handling is not productized yet.

Likely leverage points:

- [`app/lib/core/services/notification_service.dart`](app/lib/core/services/notification_service.dart)
- [`app/lib/features/profile/edit_reminder_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/profile/edit_reminder_screen.dart)
- [`app/lib/features/home/widgets/favorite_meals.dart`](app/lib/features/home/widgets/favorite_meals.dart)

### 5. Weekly Progress Recap

Create a weekly recap screen/card with calories, protein consistency, top foods, best day, missed days, and one practical goal for next week. Add a shareable image version later.

Why this matters:

- Converts raw logs into perceived progress.
- Gives users a satisfying “return moment.”
- Can power push notifications, app review prompts, and eventually social sharing.

Likely leverage points:

- Existing meal history and CSV export in [`app/lib/features/profile/settings_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/profile/settings_screen.dart)
- Existing AI summary pipeline in [`backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts`](backend/src/jobs/aiSummaryCron.ts)
- Existing chart/dashboard components in [`app/lib/features/home/widgets/`](app/lib/features/home/widgets/)

### 6. Fast Re-Log And Meal Routine Builder

Favorites already exist, but the app can go further: suggest “usual meals” based on repeated logs, allow one-tap re-log from yesterday, and let users create simple routines like weekday breakfast or post-workout snack.

Why this matters:

- Reduces logging friction for repeat users.
- Makes the app faster over time, which is core user value for calorie tracking.
- Builds on favorites and history rather than requiring a new system from scratch.

Likely leverage points:

- [`app/lib/features/home/widgets/favorite_meals.dart`](app/lib/features/home/widgets/favorite_meals.dart)
- [`app/lib/features/history/meal_history_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/history/meal_history_screen.dart)
- [`app/lib/core/providers/favorites_providers.dart`](app/lib/core/providers/favorites_providers.dart)

### 7. Account And Sync Reliability

Before building deeper retention features, decide whether the product is local-first anonymous or account-backed. Google sign-in UI exists, but the sign-in service is not active, and periodic sync is disabled. Growth features like weekly recaps, push personalization, and cross-device continuity need reliable identity and sync.

Why this matters:

- Protects user trust and data continuity.
- Enables meaningful server-side personalization.
- Reduces risk before adding more notification or recap features.

Likely leverage points:

- [`app/lib/core/services/auth_service.dart`](app/lib/core/services/auth_service.dart)
- [`app/lib/core/services/sync_service.dart`](app/lib/core/services/sync_service.dart)
- [`app/lib/core/router/app_router.dart`](app/lib/core/router/app_router.dart)

## Suggested Build Order

1. Guided first meal flow
2. Fast re-log improvements
3. Smart coaching summary
4. Adaptive reminders with deep links
5. Logging consistency/streaks
6. Weekly recap
7. Shareable progress card/referral loops

This order prioritizes direct user value first, then habit formation, then external growth loops.
