How it Works

A useful estimate in three simple, transparent steps.

1

Snap or describe

Take a photo or type what you ate in your own words—including portions, sauces, and how it was cooked.

2

AI analysis, reviewed by you

Calorify identifies ingredients, asks a quick question when something is unclear, and lets you review the result.

3

Track progress

Save the meal, follow daily calories and macros, revisit history, and reuse favorites whenever you need them.

Why Calorify?

Built for speed, useful detail, and privacy—with powerful new ways to log.

AI-Powered Analysis

Start with a photo or natural-language description. Calorify proposes ingredients and portions, resolves uncertainty with clarifying questions, and grounds nutrition estimates in USDA food data before you save.

  • Photo and text meal logging
  • Calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber
  • Clarifications for uncertain details

Smart Tracking

Build a daily calorie goal from your profile, see your calorie and macro split at a glance, and follow seven-day trends. Search and sort favorites, edit previous meals, or add a regular meal again in a couple of taps.

  • Personalized calorie targets
  • Daily summaries and seven-day trends
  • History, editing, and reusable favorites
Analytics

Health Connect

Connect only the features you want. Calorify can read today's total calories burned to adjust your daily view, and can separately share meals you log with Health Connect.

  • Optional calories-burned access
  • Optional logged-meal sharing
  • Manage either permission at any time
